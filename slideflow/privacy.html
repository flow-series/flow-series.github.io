Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

The developer values users' personal information and complies with applicable laws, including the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection and the Personal Information Protection Act. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the desktop application SlideFlow.

1. Purpose of Collecting and Using Personal Information

The app does not collect or store personal information on any external server. Data is used solely for the following service purpose:

  • Google Calendar integration: Data is processed exclusively to synchronize your Google Calendar events with the in-app timeline and calendar widget display.

2. Personal Information Collected

  • Google account authentication data: Google OAuth tokens (Access token, Refresh token)
  • Calendar data: Event titles, times, and descriptions from your Google Calendar

🔒 Important: All collected data is stored exclusively on your local device. It is never transmitted to the developer's servers or any external database.

3. Retention and Deletion of Personal Information

  • The app does not retain your personal information on any external system.
  • When you log out of your Google account within the app, or uninstall the application, all stored authentication tokens and cached calendar data on your device are immediately and permanently deleted.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The developer does not share or entrust your personal information to any third party. The app communicates directly with Google's servers through the official Google API. Any data transmission that occurs in this process is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

SlideFlow's use of data received from Google APIs, and any transfer of such data, strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Your Rights

You may withdraw consent to data collection at any time by logging out of your Google account within the app, or by uninstalling the application. Both actions result in the immediate deletion of all locally stored data.
