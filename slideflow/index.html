📸 SlideFlow — Minimalist & Intelligent Image Viewer
"The most perfect flow of hiding everything unnecessary, only immersing yourself in photography."
SlideFlow is a cross-platform (macOS / Windows)-enabled minimal image viewer designed for designers, developers, and photo enthusiasts.
Move away from the complex UI layout that fills the screen. With a single drag-and-drop, an intelligent controller that fetches images and pops up flexibly according to the mouse pointer's movement, it provides the best canvas to fully immerse yourself in the photo.

✨ Key Features
Intelligent Full-Canvas UI (Intelligent Immersive Screen)
Press the V key or the eye button on the bottom bar to completely erase the native top menu bar and title bar of macOS/Windows.
If you take the mouse to the top or bottom edge of the screen with the UI hidden, the hidden menu and control bar appear naturally and smoothly.
Seamless Slide Show
The custom slide show can be easily controlled through the one-touch play/stop button in the center of the lower control bar and the interval adjustment slider.
Responsive Image Layout Fit
The right-click context menu and the top view menu support 'Tune the image horizontally' and 'Tune the image vertically' functions. It provides the best view for the monitor environment regardless of the proportion of photos.
High-Speed Drag & Drop Parsing
Drag and drop a single photo from the Finder/Explorer to automatically collect a list of sibling images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, etc.) in that folder and navigate immediately.
Global Multi-Language Support (embedded with five languages)
It is equipped with a total of five languages, including Korean, English, Japanese, 简体, and Espan ̃, so that users around the world can use it according to their system language.
