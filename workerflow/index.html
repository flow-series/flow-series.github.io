Features

Your calendar,
reimagined as a widget.

Every feature is designed to surface your schedule without ever getting in the way.

🔗

Google Calendar Sync

Connect your Google account securely via OAuth 2.0. Schedules sync automatically every 5 minutes and instantly on app focus. Add, edit, or delete events directly from the widget.

⏱

Live Timeline View

A vertical timeline pinned to the edge of your screen shows your full day at a glance. A live red line marks the current moment. Drag on the timeline to instantly create a new event.

📆

Monthly Calendar

Switch to a full monthly overview. Click any date to jump the timeline to that day. Double-click to open a new event modal. Drag to select a date range and create a multi-day event.

🎨

Mica & Customization

Applies Windows 11 Mica material for a translucent, frosted glass background. Adjust color, transparency, and font size. Pin to top. Auto-run at startup. System tray resident.

🔒

100% Local — Your Data Never Leaves Your PC

WorkerFlow stores all data — OAuth tokens, schedule cache, local events — exclusively on your local device. Nothing is ever transmitted to an external server. Logging out or uninstalling immediately and permanently destroys all stored data.
